## 1. Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

> **"We catch GST errors before they cost you money — not after the notice arrives."**

Three pillars that differentiate this from existing tools:

1. **WhatsApp-native, zero learning curve** — no app download, no login for the end business owner. This matters enormously for the target segment (shop owners who already live on WhatsApp and resist new software).
2. **Compliance-specific intelligence, not generic OCR** — understands GSTIN validity, HSN/SAC expected rates, and ITC risk — not just text extraction. Competing OCR tools extract data; this product understands *consequences*.
3. **CA-first distribution** — sells to the person who already owns the filing relationship and has 30-200 clients, not to individual shop owners one at a time.

## 2. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor Type | Examples | Gap We Fill |
|---|---|---|
| Full accounting suites | Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks | Too complex, requires trained staff, not WhatsApp-native |
| Generic invoice OCR | Various scanner apps | No GST-specific validation logic, no reconciliation |
| GST filing software | ClearTax, Cleartax GST, Zoho GST | Focused on filing mechanics, not proactive error prevention at capture time |
| Manual (status quo) | Shoebox of paper invoices to CA monthly | What most small businesses actually do today |

**Positioning**: Not a replacement for Tally or ClearTax — a proactive error-catching layer that sits *before* filing software, feeding it clean, validated data.

## 3. Target Market Sizing (India)

- ~1.4 crore (14 million) GST-registered businesses in India
- Estimated 3-5 lakh practicing CAs/tax practitioners handling small business compliance
- Even capturing 1% of CAs (3,000-5,000) at an average of 50 clients each = a meaningful business

## 5. Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Warm network + CA communities (Months 1-3)
- CAs trust peer referrals far more than cold outreach — start with any CA contacts in your network
- Target CA associations, LinkedIn CA groups, and regional Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) chapter events
- Offer free onboarding + white-glove setup for first 20 CAs in exchange for feedback and testimonials

### Phase 2: Content + demonstrated ROI (Months 3-6)
- Publish concrete case studies: "CA X caught ₹Y in missed ITC across Z clients in one month"
- Short WhatsApp-demo videos — show the actual flow, since the product's simplicity is the selling point
- Target regional business associations and trade bodies for shop-owner segment awareness (drives inbound pressure on their CAs)

### Phase 3: Scaled acquisition (Months 6+)
- Referral program: CAs get 1 month free per referred CA who converts to paid
- Explore partnerships with GST Suvidha Providers (GSPs) who already have CA distribution channels

## 6. Key Risks to the GTM Plan

- **Trust barrier**: CAs are liable for their clients' filings — any extraction error that leads to a wrong filing is a serious trust breach. The confidence-gated review flow exists specifically to protect against this; lead with it in every sales conversation.
- **Seasonality**: GST filing has monthly/quarterly cycles — outreach timed around filing deadlines (when pain is most acute) converts better than off-cycle outreach.
- **Regulatory dependency**: GST rules change periodically (rate revisions, new compliance requirements) — the HSN/SAC rate table and validation logic need an ongoing maintenance process, not a one-time build.
