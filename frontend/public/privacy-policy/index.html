Privacy Policy for BillGuru AI

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Welcome to BillGuru AI ("we", "our", or "us"). BillGuru AI is an intelligent GST Compliance, Invoice Extraction, and Tax Reconciliation Platform designed for Chartered Accountants (CAs), small businesses, and taxpayers.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our web application and our WhatsApp Business chatbot integration.

1. Information We Collect

When you use BillGuru AI or connect via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Business Information: Shop/Business name, GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number), and registered phone number.
  • Invoice & Receipt Media: Photos, PDF documents, and transaction slips that you forward to our WhatsApp chatbot or upload via our web portal.
  • Extracted Accounting Data: Vendor names, invoice numbers, dates, taxable values, CGST, SGST, IGST amounts, and HSN codes derived through AI optical character recognition (OCR).
  • Communication Metadata: Sender phone numbers, timestamps, and message statuses delivered via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to fulfill accounting, tax compliance, and reconciliation services:

  • To extract and digitize purchase invoices and expense slips automatically.
  • To cross-reference vendor GSTINs against official tax databases to prevent invalid Input Tax Credit (ITC) claims.
  • To prepare monthly GSTR-3B and GSTR-1 summaries for your Chartered Accountant.
  • To respond to your queries on WhatsApp regarding account reconciliation status.

3. Data Protection and Zero-Sell Guarantee

  • We never sell your data: Your financial records, client names, and invoices are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Encryption: All media and messages transmitted via WhatsApp and web APIs are secured using TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Access Control: Only authorized Chartered Accountants linked to your business profile can view your invoices.

4. Third-Party Services (Meta WhatsApp Cloud API)

When interacting with our WhatsApp bot, messages and media pass through Meta Platforms, Inc. under their WhatsApp Business Terms of Service. Meta provides end-to-end transport between your WhatsApp client and our secure webhook listener.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain accounting records for the statutory retention periods required by applicable Indian tax laws (typically 6 years), or until you request deletion. You may request complete erasure of your business data at any time by contacting our support team.

6. User Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your business records, invoices, or phone number associations, send an email to support@billguru.ai with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" accompanied by your registered WhatsApp phone number and GSTIN. We will permanently delete your records within 48 business hours.
